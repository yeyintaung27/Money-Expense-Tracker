Privacy Policy

Money Expense Tracker — last updated 31 October 2026

This policy explains what the Money Expense Tracker ("the app") stores and why. The app is a personal expense-tracking tool.

What we collect

  • Your Google account email address — used only to identify your account and keep your expenses separate from everyone else's.
  • The expenses you enter — amount, date, category and optional note. These are entered by you, voluntarily.

How you sign in

Sign-in uses Google Sign-In. We request only your basic profile information and email address (the openid, email and profile scopes). We never see your Google password, and we do not access your Google Drive, Gmail, contacts, calendar or any other Google data.

Where data is stored

Your email and your expenses are stored in a Google Sheets spreadsheet owned by the app operator. Each row is tagged with your account so that your data can only be read by you. A signed-in session token (valid for 30 days) is kept in your own browser's local storage so you stay signed in.

What we do NOT do

  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking cookies.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not use your data for anything other than showing it back to you.

Your choices

  • You can delete any transaction from the Transactions page at any time.
  • You can sign out at any time from the sidebar.
  • To have your account and all of your entries removed completely, contact us at the address below and we will delete every row tagged with your email.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email expense.tracker.help@gmail.com.
